The gap we bridge

Being a legitimate seller does not mean being the right seller.

Buyers may not know which sellers truly specialize in the breed they want, what questions to ask, or who will take responsibility when promises are not kept. A seller may be legitimate and experienced with one breed — but that does not automatically make them the right source for another.

Someone known for Rottweilers may not be the right specialist to source a Pomeranian. Buyers can overlook that difference and may encounter delayed delivery, poor communication, broken promises, misleading representations, or limited follow-through.

Service fee

The Petcopaw service fee pays for Petcopaw's time, industry experience, guidance, sourcing assistance, and recommendation. It is not payment for the animal and does not guarantee that a purchase will occur.

Refund policy — full text expand_more

Full refund if Petcopaw cannot make a suitable recommendation. If Petcopaw is unable to recommend a suitable breeder for the client's requested breed, the client receives a full refund of the Petcopaw service fee.

No refund once a suitable breeder is recommended and contact is provided. Once Petcopaw recommends a suitable breeder and provides the client's contact information, Petcopaw has fulfilled its service and the service fee is non-refundable. There is no refund if the client chooses not to contact or proceed with the breeder, the breeder does not complete a sale, or the parties do not reach an agreement.

Frequently asked

Clear answers, upfront.

What is Petcopaw, and how does your pet sourcing service work in Nigeria?

expand_more

Petcopaw is an independent pet sourcing consultancy in Nigeria. We help individuals looking for dogs, cats, birds, and other companion animals identify suitable breeders or independent sellers based on the breed they want and the source's experience and specialization. With decades of experience in the pet industry and established relationships within the industry, we provide sourcing guidance, recommendations, and direct introductions. Petcopaw does not breed, sell, or own animals.

Can Petcopaw help me find a reputable dog or cat breeder in Nigeria?

expand_more

Yes. Petcopaw helps people looking for specific dog and cat breeds connect with independent breeders whose experience and specialization are relevant to their requirements. Rather than simply directing you to whoever has an animal available, we consider the breed, your expectations, and the suitability of the potential source before making a recommendation.

Does Petcopaw sell pets or operate as a pet shop?

expand_more

No. Petcopaw is not a pet shop, online pet store, or animal marketplace. We do not own, breed, advertise, or sell animals. We provide independent pet sourcing guidance and introductions to suitable breeders or sellers. Buyers discuss availability, prices, payment, and purchase arrangements directly with the independent breeder or seller.

How does Petcopaw identify suitable breeders and pet sources?

expand_more

We consider the specific breed requested, the source's relevant experience, specialization, industry relationships, and suitability for the buyer's requirements. Our recommendations are informed by our industry experience and knowledge. They are not formal certifications, veterinary inspections, or guarantees of a breeder's conduct or an animal's health.

Can Petcopaw source any dog breed, cat breed, or bird?

expand_more

Not necessarily. Our ability to make a recommendation depends on the breed, the buyer's specifications, and whether we can identify a suitable source. We do not recommend a seller simply because an animal is available. If we cannot make a suitable recommendation, our refund policy applies.

Does Petcopaw provide pet sourcing services outside Lagos?

expand_more

Petcopaw considers sourcing requests from customers across Nigeria. Your location, preferred breed, timing, and specific requirements help us assess the request and determine whether we can recommend an appropriate source. Availability depends on the breed and our ability to identify a suitable independent breeder or seller.

How much does Petcopaw charge for pet sourcing in Nigeria?

expand_more

Petcopaw charges a transparent consultancy and sourcing fee based on the type of sourcing assistance requested. Our current service fees are ₦25,000, ₦35,000, and ₦50,000 depending on the selected sourcing tier. These fees cover our time, industry experience, guidance, sourcing assistance, and recommendation. They are separate from the animal's purchase price, which is paid directly to the independent breeder or seller.

How long does it take Petcopaw to find a pet for me?

expand_more

Once payment is confirmed, Petcopaw typically completes the sourcing process and provides a suitable breeder or seller recommendation within 1-2 hours. After the introduction, the buyer communicates directly with the breeder or seller regarding availability, pricing, payment, delivery, and other purchase arrangements. Those arrangements and their timelines are outside Petcopaw's control.

What happens if Petcopaw cannot find a suitable breeder?

expand_more

If Petcopaw cannot identify and recommend a suitable breeder or seller for your requested breed, you receive a full refund of the Petcopaw service fee. Once a suitable recommendation and contact information have been provided, the sourcing service is considered fulfilled under our refund policy.
